Launchpad Wars

Round one, and he never needed a round two An arcade cabinet nobody is playing, running the same match forever

01 · OverviewWhat is Launchpad Wars

The launchpads spent the year fighting over fees and everybody watched it like a tournament. So somebody built the cabinet. Six fighters on the select screen, one green cat among them, and a machine that runs the bracket on its own with nobody standing at it.

You are not playing. The cursor picks for you, the match starts on its own, and the same cat wins the same way every time. Then the counter runs down and it starts again.

Cabinet
Attract mode only. There is no coin slot and no controls
Our fighter
Pons Cat, small, green, bandaged paws, cap with a feather
Match length
Seven hits, one knockdown, about twenty seconds
Chain
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · RosterSix on the select screen

fighters ├── The Dev hooded ships at four in the morning, never in daylight ├── The Sniper chrome one red lens, first in every block ├── The Whale heavy does not move, does not need to ├── The Jeet fast already leaving, was never really here ├── The Rug rolled carpet, tassels, two yellow eyes └── Pons Cat ours takes it from whoever has it and hands it back

The cursor walks the whole roster before it settles. It has looked at all five of the others and it has picked the cat every single time it has ever run.

03 · The matchHow it goes

Select, versus, round one, fight. The cat lunges seven times, the rug takes all seven, the combo counter climbs and the health bar on the right goes from full to nothing. The clock at the top keeps counting down and never gets anywhere near the end.

Opponent
The Rug, every time. It is the only one who agreed to the rematch
Result
Perfect. The bar on the left has never moved
After
The winner screen holds the contract, then the cabinet resets

04 · ControlsUsing the site

Nothing
There is no scrolling and nothing to click in the cabinet. It runs itself
Buy, X, Docs
Always in the top corner, on every screen of the loop
Sound
Quiet until you tap. Your first click anywhere starts a slow loop
The contract
On the winner screen at the end of every match

06 · PrivacyNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. Six portraits, four poses, one stage and a timer.

Music
One CC0 loop from OpenGameArt, plays after your first tap
Reduced motion
If your system asks for it the shake and the flash stop, the match still plays
Affiliation
None. The fighters are types, not companies. Not a game, not advice
